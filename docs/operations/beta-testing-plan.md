# Public Beta Testing Plan

**Last Updated:** 2026-06-04

This public beta-testing plan focuses on methodology, not private launch operations or participant management.

## Goals

1. Validate that users can find relevant food, housing, crisis, and community services.
2. Confirm that crisis pathways avoid AI overreach and surface emergency options.
3. Check accessibility with keyboard, screen reader, contrast, and reduced-motion needs.
4. Identify stale or confusing service data for manual review.
5. Confirm that analytics remain aggregate and privacy-conscious.

## Public Test Areas

- search relevance,
- service-detail clarity,
- offline/PWA behavior,
- multilingual UI,
- feedback submission,
- partner portal basics,
- privacy and safety copy.

## Privacy Rules

- Do not collect raw search queries from testers.
- Do not collect unnecessary personal information.
- Do not publish participant identities without explicit permission.
- Store private feedback notes outside public git.
