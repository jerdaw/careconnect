# Public Feedback Analysis Method

**Last Updated:** 2026-06-04

CareConnect feedback analysis should focus on aggregate patterns, service-data corrections, accessibility issues, and workflow friction. Private participant notes and operational details must remain outside public git.

## Public-Safe Categories

- inaccurate or stale service data,
- missing eligibility or hours information,
- search relevance gaps,
- accessibility barriers,
- confusing copy,
- partner workflow issues.

## Privacy Boundaries

- Do not publish raw personal stories without consent.
- Do not publish sensitive search text.
- Do not publish private contact details.
- Summarize patterns instead of exposing individual feedback.
