# Public Communication Principles

**Last Updated:** 2026-06-04

This public page describes communication principles. Exact incident templates, escalation contacts, private channels, and launch messaging drafts are private maintainer material.

## Principles

1. Be accurate and specific about known impact.
2. Avoid medical, legal, or official-affiliation overclaims.
3. Do not disclose private infrastructure details.
4. Do not expose user search text or personal feedback.
5. Clearly distinguish verified facts from investigation status.

## Public Message Shape

- what happened,
- who may be affected,
- what users can do now,
- what has been restored,
- where to find the next public update.
