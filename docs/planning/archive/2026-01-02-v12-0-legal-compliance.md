# Roadmap v12.0: Privacy, Safety, And Legal-Risk Infrastructure

> **Status:** Historical archive summary
> **Public boundary:** Original detailed legal/compliance planning notes were preserved privately during public GitHub cleanup.

## Purpose

This historical roadmap established the need for stronger public terms, privacy notices, accessibility work, crisis disclaimers, and data-governance processes for CareConnect.

## Public-Safe Summary

The v12 workstream focused on:

1. adding plain-language Terms of Service and Privacy Policy surfaces,
2. documenting that CareConnect is a directory, not an emergency, medical, legal, or counselling service,
3. improving crisis-safety disclaimers and routing,
4. strengthening accessibility documentation and testing,
5. creating privacy/security incident preparation,
6. preparing governance material for future partner review.

## Current Caveat

This archive does not claim formal PIPEDA, PHIPA, AODA, GDPR, or other legal compliance. It records risk-reduction work and documentation intent. Formal legal/privacy determinations require qualified review.
