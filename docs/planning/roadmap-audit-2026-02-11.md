# Roadmap Audit Summary - 2026-02-11

This public summary preserves the technical and governance outcomes from the February 2026 roadmap audit while excluding private personal strategy and application-planning content.

## Scope

The audit reviewed CareConnect's:

- service-data quality posture,
- search and privacy architecture,
- accessibility and multilingual readiness,
- production-readiness gaps,
- governance and verification workflows,
- testing and documentation coverage.

## Public Findings

1. CareConnect needed clearer separation between prototype claims, pilot readiness, and production operations.
2. Public claims needed to remain evidence-bound, especially for privacy, accessibility, service freshness, and impact.
3. Data quality work was more valuable when scoped to pilot-relevant services instead of broad, unprioritized expansion.
4. Search quality, crisis behavior, and privacy boundaries were the most important user-facing reliability surfaces.
5. Deployment and monitoring details needed a clearer public/private documentation boundary.

## Follow-Through

The audit fed into later v20-v22 work:

- technical hardening and test coverage,
- search explainability,
- privacy-safe analytics,
- stale-data governance,
- pilot metric instrumentation,
- public-claim hardening,
- documentation boundary cleanup.

## Boundary Note

Personal strategy, private operational plans, host-specific deployment details, and exact incident-response procedures are intentionally excluded from this public summary.
