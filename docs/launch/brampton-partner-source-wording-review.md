# Brampton Partner And Source Wording Review

Date: 2026-07-08
Status: draft-only wording review; no new public relationship claims approved

## Publication Gate

Do not publish copy that implies a provider, municipality, regional government, provincial body, Indigenous organization, directory, or community organization operates, endorses, sponsors, approves, or partners with CareConnect unless that relationship is documented and explicitly approved.

Source links can support manual review. They are not partner claims.

## Current Public Baseline

The existing partner/source page frames sources as reference inputs for manual review. Current English copy states that reference sources guide curation and are not presented as endorsements or official partnerships unless confirmed.

This baseline remains the recommended public posture for Brampton.

## Safe Language

Use:

- `reference source`
- `public source`
- `source reviewed for manual verification`
- `helps guide curation`
- `used to compare public contact details`
- `not an endorsement or official partnership unless confirmed`
- `provider corrections and verification are welcome`

Example:

> CareConnect uses public provider, directory, municipal, regional, and community sources as inputs for manual review. A source link does not mean the source endorses, operates, or partners with CareConnect.

Example:

> Brampton records are added only after review. Public source links help reviewers confirm contact details, intake paths, and service coverage.

## Unsafe Language

Avoid unless a documented relationship is approved:

- `official partner`
- `partnered with the City of Brampton`
- `endorsed by Peel Region`
- `approved by`
- `in collaboration with`
- `in partnership with`
- `verified by the municipality`
- provider logos used as proof of relationship
- municipal or regional branding used as a partner badge
- Indigenous source references framed as endorsement or consultation

## Brampton Source Reference Rules

- A City of Brampton, Peel Region, 211, provider, or Indigenous-governance URL may be cited as a public source only.
- Public pages can support L1 review but do not create L2/L3 status by themselves.
- Provider logos should not be added for Brampton launch references without permission.
- Municipal or regional names should describe the source being reviewed, not an affiliation.
- If a provider asks to correct or verify a record, record that through the update/verification workflow before changing relationship labels.

## Approval-Ready Copy Options

### Source Page Intro

> CareConnect uses public and community reference sources to support manual review. These sources help reviewers check service details, but they do not imply endorsement, operation, sponsorship, or partnership.

### Brampton Expansion Note

> Brampton coverage begins with a small reviewed launch set. Public provider, directory, municipal, and regional pages are used as review inputs while records move through CareConnect verification.

### Provider Correction Invitation

> Providers and community organizations can submit corrections or verification notes through the update process. Confirmed provider review is recorded separately from public source review.

## Definition Of Done For Publication

- [ ] Exact wording approved by a human reviewer.
- [ ] Wording does not imply endorsement, operation, sponsorship, or partnership.
- [ ] Logos or organization names are not used as relationship claims.
- [ ] Any confirmed relationship has documented evidence outside public source links.
- [ ] Translations are prepared for all supported locales if wording is added to public UI.
