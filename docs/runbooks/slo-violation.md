# Runbook Summary: SLO Violation

**Last Updated:** 2026-06-04

This public summary explains service-level objective violations without exposing alert thresholds, dashboards, or private incident procedures.

## Meaning

An SLO violation indicates that reliability, latency, or availability is outside the project's provisional operating target.

## Public Response Principles

1. Determine user-visible impact.
2. Prioritize restoration of public search and service-detail access.
3. Avoid unsupported public uptime or compliance claims.
4. Review whether targets remain appropriate for the pilot stage.
5. Store detailed incident notes outside public git.
